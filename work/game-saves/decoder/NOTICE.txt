ooz-bytes.mjs contains the unmodified WebAssembly bytecode extracted from
package/build/ooz.js in the upstream ooz-wasm 2.0.0 release. Pal Atlas uses
its own bounded loader in save-meta.mjs. No proprietary game DLL is bundled.
